About Me

  • Hi, My name is Charlie Blaker, and I'm a 3D Technical/Shader Artist for Environments! I work a lot in Unreal Engine but I have a good amount of general 3D pipeline skills with a whole jumble of coding and technical knowledge as well!

Whether it be visual scripting in Unreal or Blender, or coding directly in a shader language, the technical problem solving combined with the artistic direction really appeals to my brain. I like to go off on a lot of tangents and end up following whatever technical rabbit hole grabs my interest first. (Often regardless of whether or not that rabbit hole will be useful...).
